Dan Schaupner: 25+ Years of Emerging Technology Transformation

Professional insights from someone who has repeatedly moved adopters from ideation to operation

This is the homepage of Dan Schaupner. I've worked in cybersecurity—SOCs, testing, architecture, and leadership—but looking back, what I really did was help customers adopt new technologies. Those customers included the U.S. government, banks, manufacturers, healthcare providers, and financial firms.

Adopting new technology takes more than cybersecurity. It involves redefining roles, developing processes and training people, forging relationships, negotiating, presenting evidence for leadership decisions, and personally bridging gaps in roles and duties. This held true across dot-com, virtualization, mobility, and cloud, and is true for AI. (See article: Transformation Pros Know: AI Remaps the Work.)

As we enter the next transformation era, I have thoughts to share. This is my personal platform, and the thoughts, experiences, and insights are my own.

I'm an instructor in the University of Cincinnati's School of Information Technology and an associate director at one of its institutes. What I post here is independent of that work and does not represent the view of UC.

But I rigorously defend intellectual and academic freedom and exercise it here.
