01 / The moment

Before the meeting decides it

You may be in that moment now.

03 / The presence

Experienced judgment, in the room

Not another advocate.

My role is not to predict the future, defend a department, or oppose the proposal.

I connect technical conditions to operating and financial consequences. I test incomplete assumptions, distinguish evidence from confidence, make dependencies visible, and define how the eventual outcome will be validated.

The answer may be proceed. It may also be narrow, sequence, correct, delay, bring in a specialist, make a necessary hire, or stop.

What changes / 01

The question becomes precise.

Leadership is clear about what it is deciding—and what it is not.

What changes / 02

Confidence is tested by evidence.

Known facts are separated from assumptions, claims, and unresolved conditions.

What changes / 03

Dependencies become visible.

Technology, people, process, authority, cost, and operating readiness are considered together.

What changes / 04

Validation is designed before approval.

The organization knows how it will determine whether the promised value was actually obtained.
