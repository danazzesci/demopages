02 / The decision

Before committing more

Clarity first.

A permanent hire or a major consultancy may eventually be right. Neither is always the right first move.

Hiring takes time. A large engagement can bring more cost and machinery than the initiative requires. Continuing as before preserves the conditions that allowed it to stall.

I

Hire

Months to search, onboard, and discover whether the fit is right.

II

Engage at scale

Useful when warranted—but expensive before the condition is clear.

III

Intervene with focus

Establish the condition, identify the constraint, and define the practical next move.

I enter the space between what was intended and what is actually operating.
03 / Where I enter

Diagnosis to movement

See clearly. Organize deliberately. Finish.

I establish the actual condition, recognize the pattern behind the impasse, and organize what must move.

That means aligning the responsible people, technical assets, decisions, risks, and operating processes—then giving management decision-ready options grounded in evidence.

The objective is not another methodology. It is to determine why this initiative has not moved, what will permit it to move now, and how success will be validated.

05 / Start small

A bounded beginning

Clarity before commitment.

The initial engagement is deliberately modest: enough access to establish what is happening and determine what would permit the initiative to move.

Establish

Understand the intended outcome and current condition.

Diagnose

Identify the technical, organizational, financial, or risk constraint.

Recommend

Present a practical path, decision, and definition of done.

The result may be

A focused correction. A recovery plan. Specialist support. A defined hire. A larger implementation. Or the informed decision to redesign, transfer, or stop.
