Example: explaining your AI use

This is an illustration of the disclosure process, not an answer to the case. Describe what you actually did; do not copy checks you did not perform.

Tool and model
“I used [tool name] with [model name/version shown in the tool] on [date].” If the model was not shown, say so rather than guessing.
What I asked and what I supplied
“I asked AI to help organize my reasoning. I supplied [specific article passages, source titles, page numbers, or links]. I asked it to separate source-supported claims from assumptions.” State whether it could access the sources or whether you pasted excerpts.
What AI contributed
“It suggested an outline and clarified a term. I rewrote the explanation and supported each important claim with a source I checked.” Explain the contribution to your own answer.
Evidence and checks for invented claims
“I opened the original sources and checked that the cited passages existed and supported the claims. I compared technical claims with [a named independent source or official documentation]. I removed [an unsupported claim] and corrected [an error]. I did not treat AI-generated citations or an AI self-check as verification.” Include the checks you actually performed. Asking AI not to hallucinate does not guarantee accuracy.
Confidence and remaining uncertainty
“My confidence is [High / Medium / Low] because [specific evidence and checks]. I could not verify [detail], so I marked it as uncertain rather than presenting it as fact.” Confidence should reflect the evidence, not how convincing the AI sounded.
