Why eight housings are shown
The top-down view suggests several separate enclosed interface channels rather than one exposed PLC rack. This model assigns three housings to sensor inputs, three to pump outputs, one to the FT valve, and one to common power, communications, or a spare channel. The exact function of each housing remains an informed reconstruction until labels or internal wiring are visible.
Thumbscrew junctions
The rear junctions are modeled as field termination points. Short internal wires connect each housing to a thumbscrew terminal, while longer harness wires descend behind the cart to sensors, pumps, and the valve actuator. This gives the trainer a clean disconnect and troubleshooting boundary.
Automatic sequence
Tank A pumps into B. When B reaches its threshold, B pumps into C. When C reaches its threshold, C returns the water to A. Opening the ABC-to-FT valve pauses the automatic sequence and drains all three process tanks into the lower reservoir.
