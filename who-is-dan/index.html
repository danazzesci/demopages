Who is Dan Schaupner?

click here for current speaking topics

Dan Schaupner is recognized as a cybersecurity expert and has been called on as a hands-on implementer of cybersecurity tools and systems, consultant for management and planning, and speaker about emerging topics worldwide.

But the important part of his story is that he has engaged, led, and guided the adoption of emerging technology across more than 25 years and multiple technology eras. The intense need for cybersecurity afforded him a front-row seat to that change.

He has personally engineered, integrated, tested, and operated systems. He has briefed senior management and moved leaders to action. He has led technical and consulting organizations and developed policy and operating models for government and industry.

Dan has now adapted those experiences to the AI era, including hands-on AI application development, AI training and teaching, and advising on AI strategy, workforce transformation, governance, testing, and policy.

Today, he is an adjunct professor and an associate director of a cybersecurity institute at a major university in Ohio. He continues to engage directly with technical and leadership communities through speaking, industry engagement, course development, early-learner intervention spanning K–12 and college, and technical working groups.

Dan is a graduate of the University of Michigan College of Engineering and the Virginia Tech Pamplin College of Business, where he earned his MBA.

The technology changes. The problem remains remarkably consistent: how do you turn emerging technology into an organizational capability that actually works?
