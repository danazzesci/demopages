About This Page

This site is the public, professional presence of Dan Schaupner. It is a place for me to share views, news, perspectives, and specialized content on the effective and controlled adoption of AI—and on adapting to an environment where AI is becoming an assumed part of how we work, rather than the exception.

One perspective for your consideration

My observations are offered as one input for readers seeking to learn more about operating AI and working in an emerging AI environment. They are informed by my research, experience, and previous business cases.

My views, independently expressed

The views expressed on this site are my own. They do not represent the views or positions of my current or previous employers, the University of Cincinnati, my clients, or any other person or organization.

Where I cite others, their views remain their own and should be understood in the context of the original sources referenced. A citation does not imply endorsement of my views.

By invitation only

“By invitation only” means just that: some messages and materials are private, intended for limited audiences, and shared only with invited participants.

I am excited to share advanced or more fully developed editions of my topics with invited parties.
